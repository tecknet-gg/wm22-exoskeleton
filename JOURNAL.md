## 16th September - 1.5 hours

I started of designing the exoskeleton today. Simple enough in principle - it has two rails and the walkman slides into it, exposing the front so the cassette can still 
be viewed - and leave enough space on the sides so for the control to be accessed. I grabbed attemted to find a 3D model of the WM-22 quickly - but they were all age walled
behind Sketchfab, and I couldn't be bothered bypassing that, so I just started with a slightly oversized cuboid with the dimensions of the WM-22.

I then hollowed it out and modeled the rails: 

<img width="720" height="655" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b91369ce-8a1f-482e-8dd6-3f1a68cb4c4d" />

I then added a bunch of cuts and filleted them to both save material (in the cases of the back and bottom), and to make space for the controls on the side.

And then the last thing remaining was the belt clip. I spent a moment on this part trying to shape it using splines but after like 20 minutes I realised that was just stupid, so instead I just used 
an arc and a bunch of straight lines, then offset it and extruded.

<img width="855" height="650" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/73058f67-b297-408c-92e8-676638c01696" />

And with that we're done with V1! I sent that off to be printed on my A1. I would ideally print it out of PETG, but I don't have any on hand so PLA will have to do :]

After the gruelling 4 hour print - I tried slotting in my WM-22, and ran into some issues. First of all the clip was far too low down on the body, so the body of the Walkman protruded above my 
hip, which is kinda weird. Second - it doesn't it unless you really give it a shove down there, because of the little support bar I put down the middle of the side rails. And also the cutout on the main body
interfered with the controls, so I snapped that off for now. Back to designing :]

## 17th September - 45 minutes

Not much to say - all very similar to the first version with some minor tweaks.

<img width="672" height="748" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d64383ae-c8cb-457d-90b2-09d8a4cc9d2d" />

The back cutout is now at the bottom to make space for the clip at the top. The side cutout goes around the back to make space for the controls. And yeah thats pretty much it lol. 
Clip is much of the same but moved up. 

<img width="485" height="580" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e398e4f0-d26c-4ac2-92e1-5f9441f76fb0" />

That's the final design I landed on. I got that printed - it had a bit too much slop and I realised I was a bit to generous on the side cutout. The second print was perfect in all ways - 
except it was too tight for the walkman, and finally after one last tweak - on the fourth print all was perfect, and the walkman was about as snug as a bug in a rug :].
